Lab grammar

The projects look different because their problems are different. Their engineering discipline is not.

Evidence before inference.Raw observations and receipts stay distinct from interpretation. If it was not measured, it is not quietly promoted to fact.
Systems remain inspectable.Architecture, traces, provenance and failure boundaries are part of the product surface.
Limits are first-class.Unavailable hardware, uncertain mappings and synthetic behavior are labeled rather than disguised.
A NOTE FROM THE LAB
Build things that can be looked inside.
Measure what matters.
Leave the evidence behind.
