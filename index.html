01 / OBSERVE / POSITIONING SYSTEMS
HORIZON Observatory
MEASUREMENT + ORBITAL STATE
An Android positioning observatory built around the boundary between measurement and propagation. Horizon captures GNSS, cellular and inertial observations, preserves timing and provenance, separates measured state from propagated state, and connects live observations with ephemerides, TLE/OMM data and SGP4/SDP4 propagation across TEME, ECI and ECEF reference frames. Azimuth, elevation, C/N₀, Doppler, range, state vectors, orbital state and visibility remain evidence-bearing quantities rather than decorative numbers.

02 / ENGINEERING GRAMMAR

The interfaces change. The discipline does not.

Evidence before inference.Observed data, derived state and interpretation are separate layers. Claims earn their confidence through receipts.
Provenance is part of the product.Where a value came from, when it was observed, and how it was transformed should remain inspectable.
Failure boundaries stay visible.Missing hardware, uncertain mappings, synthetic inputs and unavailable evidence are represented—not disguised.
