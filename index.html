Five systems remain private; QUANTA2 is the public, MIT-licensed embedded wave-simulation project. Across all six, the common layer is disciplined observation: acquire the signal, preserve its context, model the state, and make the boundary visible.

01 / OBSERVE / POSITIONING SYSTEMS
HORIZON Observatory
MEASUREMENT + ORBITAL STATE
◆PRIVATE
An Android scientific observatory for GNSS measurement, satellite-orbit analysis and live sky visualization. Horizon keeps raw observations, derived states and propagated orbital states distinguishable, with timestamped provenance throughout the acquisition and analysis pipeline. Its confirmed operational capabilities include GNSS measurements and PVT/status correlation; CCSDS OMM/TLE-driven SGP4/SDP4 propagation for near-Earth and deep-space orbits; TEME → ECEF → local ENU transforms; topocentric azimuth, elevation, slant range, range-rate/Doppler and geometric visibility; orientation-aware sky plotting; and session persistence, replay/export and bounded location-continuity handling. Model assumptions and accuracy limits remain explicit rather than hidden behind the UI.

02 / MODEL / COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS
QUANTA2
EMBEDDED WAVE SIMULATION
PUBLIC · MIT
An experimental classical numerical model of one-dimensional quantum wave-packet behaviour, rendered on an Arduino UNO R3 (ATmega328P) with a 128x64 I2C OLED. Its eight scenarios explore tunnelling, resonance, quantum wells, potential steps, an oscillator, Bragg structures, disorder and a quantum bouncer. A 128-cell complex state, integer-based sequential time updates and a 64-point momentum-space FFT drive the on-device visualisation. QUANTA2 is a microcontroller-hosted simulation - not a quantum computer and not a qubit device.

02 / ENGINEERING GRAMMAR

The interfaces change. The discipline does not.

Evidence before inference.Observed data, derived state and interpretation are separate layers. Claims earn their confidence through receipts.
Provenance is part of the product.Where a value came from, when it was observed, and how it was transformed should remain inspectable.
Failure boundaries stay visible.Missing hardware, uncertain mappings, synthetic inputs and unavailable evidence are represented—not disguised.
